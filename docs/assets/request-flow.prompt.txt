Use case: ads-marketing.
Asset type: a wide hero artwork for Ferry, a serious developer tool that routes AI requests, queues concurrency, waits through rate limits, and switches failed upstreams.
CRITICAL: this is abstract SOFTWARE infrastructure. Absolutely no boats, harbors, bridges, rivers, water, buildings, scenery, vehicles, cargo or physical travel metaphors. No logo or typography.
Art direction: exceptional computational graphic design, disciplined Swiss editorial composition with fine generative linework. Matte near-black graphite background. A sculptural ribbon made of dozens of hairline warm-white luminous paths moves confidently from left to right, widening and gathering into an elegant asymmetric S-curve. Around its middle, a restrained amber segment of one secondary path is paused as a few orderly small capsules; the dominant flowing ribbon takes a graceful alternate bend and continues uninterrupted to a small icy mint termination accent. This suggests continuity, scheduling, recovery and intelligent routing without becoming a literal diagram.
Style: mostly monochrome, precise and minimal, a subtle dimensional depth to the interwoven paths, exquisitely controlled highlights, micro-fine silk-screen grain, tension between mathematical order and organic flow. Strong negative space. Refined professional developer-brand art, not flashy cyberpunk. One visual gesture, not scattered symbols.
Composition: panoramic 3:1, intended around 1800x600. Full artwork visible with generous margins, visually balanced across a README banner, impressive at full size and legible as an abstract silhouette at 800px wide.
Avoid: text, logos, arrows, UI screenshots, charts, rectangles full of tiny text, floating robots, brains, generic connected-dot network, server racks, hexagons, lens flare, rainbow/purple glow, stock 3D cubes. No literal ferry.
